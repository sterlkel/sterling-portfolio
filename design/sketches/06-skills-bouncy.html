Same marquee, but alive: scrolling the page speeds it up and leans it in your scroll direction, chips wobble like jelly on hover, and clicking makes one hop.

B · echoes the hero shockwave

Ripple grid

Tiles spring in one by one with an overshoot. Click any tile and a bounce ripples outward through the grid from that point — same idea as the hero click.

C · macOS dock

Dock

Hover magnifies icons with a springy falloff to their neighbors; click one and it does the "launching app" bounce. Instantly readable, a little cheeky.

D · springs, not gravity

Spring cloud

Chips float in a loose cloud, gently bobbing. Your cursor shoves them aside and they boing back into place on springs. Click to blast them all outward.
