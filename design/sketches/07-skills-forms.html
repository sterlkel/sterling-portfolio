A · die-cut stickers

Stickers on the lid

Skills as mismatched die-cut stickers — circles, hexes, stars, labels — that slap onto a laptop lid one by one. Drag them around; click to make one wiggle. Very personal, very fun.

B · mechanical keyboard

Keycaps

Each skill is a chunky keycap with real key travel and a springy return. Hover presses it; your actual keyboard presses the key that starts with that letter.

try typing on your keyboard
C · bubbles sized by experience

Bubbles

Bubble size = how much I use it. They drift, jostle each other and wobble. Push them with your cursor; click to pop one and watch it re-inflate.

D · type is the shape

Kinetic type

No containers at all — skills as one big run of text. Hover a word and its weight surges from thin to black while each letter hops in sequence. Editorial and bouncy at once.

E · ties back to the alien hero

Goo

Skills as liquid blobs that orbit and merge into each other like a lava lamp. Your cursor is a blob too — drag it through and they fuse to it.
